# Madhava Mathematics Competition — Solved Papers & Comprehensive Solutions

Welcome to the **Madhava Mathematics Competition Solved Papers & Preparation Toolkit** archive! This repository is dedicated to providing undergraduate students and competitive mathematics aspirants with rigorous, beginner-friendly, and comprehensive step-by-step solutions, theoretical masterclasses, formula cheat sheets, and practice problem sets for the **Madhava Mathematics Competition**.

The competition is organized annually by **Bhaskaracharya Pratishthana, Pune** in collaboration with the Department of Mathematics, S. P. College, Pune, and is funded by the **National Board for Higher Mathematics (NBHM)**.

## 🎯 4-Step Roadmap to Score Full Marks

1. **Step 1 — Master Theory with the Concepts Guide:**
   - Read [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Concepts_Masterclass.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Concepts_Masterclass.pdf).
   - Understand the theoretical foundations: $p$-adic valuations, Infinite Descent, Hilbert Matrices, Rouché's Theorem, Commutator Nilpotency, Auxiliary Functions for Rolle's Theorem, and Topological Connectedness.
2. **Step 2 — Memorize Key Identities with the Cheat Sheet:**
   - Review [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Formula_CheatSheet.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Formula_CheatSheet.pdf).
   - Memorize polygon triangle counts ($N_0 = \frac{n(n-4)(n-5)}{6}$), root bounding conditions, and commutator power identities on a compact 2-page printout.
3. **Step 3 — Reinforce with the Twin Drill Problem Set:**
   - Solve the 18 problems in [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Practice_Drills.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Practice_Drills.pdf).
   - Use the **Tiered Hints** (Hint 1 for strategy, Hint 2 for algebraic trigger) before consulting the full solutions.
4. **Step 4 — Review the Official Paper & Marking Rubrics:**
   - Benchmark your solutions against [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Detailed_Solutions.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Detailed_Solutions.pdf) to ensure full marks under official examiner rubrics.
