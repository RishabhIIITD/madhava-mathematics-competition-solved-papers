## Part I
**N.B. Each question in Part I carries 2 marks.**

1. Let $a < b < c$ be three positive integers in geometric progression whose common ratio is also an integer. If the arithmetic mean of $a, b, c$ is $b + 2$, then the value of $a$ is:  
   **(A)** $24$  
   **(B)** $12$  
   **(C)** $6$  
   **(D)** $4$  

2. Triangles are formed by joining the vertices of a regular polygon of $12$ sides. The number of triangles having no side in common with the polygon is:  
   **(A)** $124$  
   **(B)** $220$  
   **(C)** $96$  
   **(D)** $112$  

3. If 
   $$f(x) = \begin{cases} ax + b & \text{if } x < -1 \\ x^2 + 1 & \text{if } -1 \le x < 1 \\ 3x - 1 & \text{if } x \ge 1 \end{cases}$$
   is continuous everywhere, then which of the following values can $ab$ never take?  
   **(A)** $-3$  
   **(B)** $-1$  
   **(C)** $0$  
   **(D)** $3$  

4. Let $f : [1, 3] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. If $f$ takes rational values for all $x$ and $f(2) = 10$, then $f(1.5) =$  
   **(A)** $7.5$  
   **(B)** $10$  
   **(C)** $1.5$  
   **(D)** $5$  

5. If $f(x) = (x + 1)^2 - 1, x \ge -1$, then the set $\{x : f(x) = f^{-1}(x)\}$ is:  
   **(A)** $\phi$  
   **(B)** $\{-1, 1\}$  
   **(C)** $\{0, 1\}$  
   **(D)** $\{-1, 0\}$  

6. The number of real solutions of $1 + |e^x - 1| = e^x(e^x - 2)$ is:  
   **(A)** $1$  
   **(B)** $2$  
   **(C)** $0$  
   **(D)** $3$  

7. In how many ways can you choose an odd number of objects from $n$ objects?  
   **(A)** $2^{n-1}$  
   **(B)** $2^n$  
   **(C)** $2^n - 1$  
   **(D)** $n$  

8. The family of circles with center $(a, 0)$ and radius $a$ satisfies the differential equation:  
   **(A)** $\frac{dy}{dx} + x^2 - y^2 = 0$  
   **(B)** $2xy \frac{dy}{dx} + x^2 - y^2 = 0$  
   **(C)** $2xy \frac{dy}{dx} + x - y = 0$  
   **(D)** $2xy \frac{dy}{dx} + x^2 + y^2 = 0$  

9. The series 
   $$\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{\cos(x^{2n}) + \sin(x^{2n+1}) - e^{-|x|n}}{1 + n^2}$$
   **(A)** converges pointwise everywhere on $\mathbb{R}$, but not uniformly  
   **(B)** does not converge at any point  
   **(C)** converges uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$  
   **(D)** converges only at $0$  

10. If $1, a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{n-1}$ are the $n^{\text{th}}$ ($n \ge 2$) roots of unity, then
   $$\frac{1}{1 - a_1} + \frac{1}{1 - a_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{1 - a_{n-1}} =$$
   **(A)** $\frac{n}{2}$  
   **(B)** $\frac{n - 1}{2}$  
   **(C)** $\frac{1}{2}$  
   **(D)** $\frac{n(n - 1)}{2}$

## Part II
**N.B. Each question in Part II carries 6 marks.**

1. Let $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function with 
   $$\int_0^1 x(x - 1)^2 f(x) dx = 0$$
   Prove that there is a real number $c \in (0, 1)$ such that
   $$\int_0^c x^2 f(x) dx = c \int_0^c x f(x) dx$$

2. Let $f(x) = x^2 + 4x + 3$ and $g(x) = 2x + 5$. Show that $\gcd(f(n), g(n)) = 1$ or $3$ as $n$ varies over integers.

3. Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ be real numbers. Prove that
   $$\sum_{1 \le i, j \le n} \frac{a_i a_j}{i + j - 1} \ge 0$$

4. Suppose for $n \ge 4$, $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ are non-negative integers such that after removing any one of them the remaining numbers can be partitioned into two sets with equal sums.
   1. If $n$ is even, then prove that $x_i = 0$, for all $i$.
   2. Does the same conclusion hold when $n$ is odd? Justify your answer.

5. Give an example of a function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, if it exists, which is continuous at $a$ if and only if $a$ is a positive integer. Justify your answer.

## Part III
**N.B. Marks for individual subparts are shown in brackets.**

1. Suppose $\alpha, \beta$ are roots of the polynomial $x^2 + bx + c$. Let $p > 2$ be a prime satisfying the conditions: $p$ divides $b$, $p$ divides $c$, but $p^2$ does not divide $c$.
   1. Find the largest power of $p$ which divides $\alpha^4 + \beta^4$. **[2]**
   2. Find the largest power of $p$ which divides $\alpha^8 + \beta^8$. **[3]**
   3. For any positive integer $n$, find the largest power of $p$ which divides $\alpha^{2^n} + \beta^{2^n}$. **[8]**

2. 
   1. Show that $x^6 - 6x^5 + 1 = 0$ has a unique root in the interval $(0, 1)$. **[3]**
   2. Show that every complex (non-real) root $z$ of $x^6 - 6x^5 + 1 = 0$ satisfies $|z| \le x_0$, where $x_0$ is the root of this equation in $(0, 1)$. **[10]**

3. 
   1. Suppose $A, B$ are $n \times n$ complex matrices with $AB^2 - B^2 A = B$. Show that $B$ is a nilpotent matrix. **[9]**
   2. Let $B$ be an $n \times n$ complex nilpotent matrix. Does there exist an $n \times n$ matrix $A$ such that $AB^2 - B^2 A = B$? Justify your answer. **[3]**

4. Let $A, B, C, D$ be vertices of a quadrilateral inscribed in the unit circle. Let $P$ be a point in the interior of the quadrilateral $ABCD$. Show that
   $$|PA||PB||PC||PD| < \frac{27}{16}$$
   where $|PX|$ is the length of the line segment $PX$. **[12]**
