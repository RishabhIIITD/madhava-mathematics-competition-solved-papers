# Madhava Mathematics Competition — Solved Papers & Comprehensive Preparation Archive

Welcome to the **Madhava Mathematics Competition Solved Papers & Complete Preparation Archive**! This repository is dedicated to providing undergraduate students and competitive mathematics aspirants with official past question papers, official marking schemes, rigorous beginner-friendly detailed solutions, theoretical masterclasses, formula cheat sheets, and practice drill sets for the **Madhava Mathematics Competition**.

The competition is organized annually by **Bhaskaracharya Pratishthana, Pune** in collaboration with the Department of Mathematics, S. P. College, Pune, and is funded by the **National Board for Higher Mathematics (NBHM)**.

## 🎯 Recommended 5-Step Study Workflow to Score Full Marks

1. **Step 1 — Build Conceptual Foundations:**
   - Read [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Concepts_Masterclass.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Concepts_Masterclass.pdf).
   - Master the theory: $p$-adic valuations, Infinite Descent, Hilbert Matrices, Rouché's Theorem, Commutator Nilpotency, Auxiliary Functions for Rolle's Theorem, and Connectedness.
2. **Step 2 — Memorize Key Identities with the Cheat Sheet:**
   - Review [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Formula_CheatSheet.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Formula_CheatSheet.pdf).
   - Memorize polygon triangle counts ($N_0 = \frac{n(n-4)(n-5)}{6}$), root bounding conditions, and commutator power identities on a compact 2-page printout.
3. **Step 3 — Take the 3-Hour Timed Mock Exam:**
   - Print [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Question_Paper.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Question_Paper.pdf).
   - Attempt the full 100-mark paper without any reference material within the strict 3-hour limit.
4. **Step 4 — Benchmark Against Official Rubrics & Detailed Solutions:**
   - Check your grade using [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Official_Solutions_and_Scheme_of_Marking.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Official_Solutions_and_Scheme_of_Marking.pdf).
   - Study alternative approaches and strategic insights in [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Detailed_Solutions.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Detailed_Solutions.pdf).
5. **Step 5 — Reinforce with the Twin Drill Problem Set:**
   - Solve the 18 problems in [`Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Practice_Drills.pdf`](./2026/Madhava_Mathematics_Competition_2026_Practice_Drills.pdf).
   - Use the **Tiered Hints** before checking the self-contained proofs in Section C.
